Your Leads Are Going Cold, And It's Not Your Fault

Someone finds you, follows you, fills out your form. You send a follow-up. Maybe two. Then life gets busy and that lead disappears.

It's not because they weren't interested. It's because there was nothing in place to keep the conversation going after that first touch. Most service businesses lose leads they already worked to attract, not from lack of effort, but because consistent follow-up is impossible to do manually at any real scale.

A structured system fixes that. One that runs in the background, sends the right message at the right time, and keeps leads warm until they're ready to buy.

The Process

A System Built Around Your Customer Journey

Every engagement follows a clear, repeatable process, from discovery through delivery, so you always know what's happening and why.

01

Discovery & Mapping

I audit your lead flow, map your full customer journey, and identify where leads fall off. You get a visual flowchart before I build anything.

02

Copywriting & Sequence Build

I write every email in your brand voice, structured for each journey stage, then build everything inside GoHighLevel.

03

Automation Setup

Every trigger, sequence transition, and behavioral path is configured and tested. When a lead comes in, the system takes over completely.

04

Handoff

I walk you through what was built and why. You get documentation explaining your system, plus two weeks of post-launch support or ongoing support if you choose a retainer.

Deliverable: A fully automated system that guides leads from first contact to close, without you manually following up on a single one.
