Who They Are

Profile is a behavioral analytics company that helps businesses, athletic programs, and educational institutions understand the people inside their organizations and convert them into engaged customers, athletes, and team members. Their tools turn behavioral data into actionable insights for coaches, executives, and L&D teams.

Profile's platform is used by organizations including
Nike · Amazon · US Army · Auburn University · Big 10 Conference

The Situation

Profile had leads coming in across multiple channels but no structured email follow-up.

Contacts who showed interest weren't being nurtured. New users weren't being onboarded systematically. Customers weren't being re-engaged when activity dropped off.

The team needed a complete customer journey, not just a few campaign emails. They needed every stage of the lifecycle covered, from initial contact through long-term engagement, running automatically inside the platform they were already using.

How I Approached It

Before writing a single email, I mapped out Profile's full customer journey: who their leads are, what triggers their interest, what their decision process looks like, and where the natural drop-off points fall. That mapping became the structural foundation for everything that came after.

From there, I designed five sequences to cover every stage of the journey, wrote each one in Profile's brand voice, and built the entire system inside GoHighLevel.

The customer journey map: from contact form submission through every decision point in the lifecycle.

What Was Built

Five sequences covering the full customer lifecycle, each connected through behavior-based logic so contacts move through the journey based on what they actually do, not on a fixed schedule.

  • 01

    Contact Form Sequence

    Initial nurture and qualification for new leads

  • 02

    Free Trial Onboarding

    Drives free trial users toward activation and conversion

  • 03

    Ongoing Engagement

    Long-term nurture for leads who haven't converted yet

  • 04

    Post-Purchase Onboarding

    Welcome and value-driving for new paying customers

  • 05

    Reactivation

    Re-engages trial users who didn't become full customers

The workflow logic built in GoHighLevel: tag-based branching routes contacts into the right sequence based on their behavior.
